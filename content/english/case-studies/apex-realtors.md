## The business

**Apex Realtors** is a Houston, TX real estate brokerage. Like most small real estate teams, they live and die by speed-to-lead: the faster an inbound call or web inquiry turns into a booked showing, the more deals they close. But their team was on the phone, in appointments, or off-hours — and every missed call was a missed commission.

## The challenge

- Inbound calls frequently went to voicemail after hours and on weekends
- Leads from Zillow, the website, and referrals sat unqualified in the CRM
- Agents spent time on reception instead of closing
- No consistent way to capture, score, and route new opportunities

## What TeckAid built

We delivered two connected AI systems:

### 1. Voice reception service

A natural-sounding voice agent that answers every inbound call in Apex's brand voice, any time of day. It greets the caller, captures property interest and contact details, answers common questions, and either transfers to an agent or books the appointment.

### 2. Custom lead-gen agent

A lead-generation agent that ingests inquiries from the website, portals, and calls, qualifies them against Apex's criteria, scores urgency, and pushes a structured record into the CRM — with a summary and next-step for the assigned agent.

## How it fits together

```
Inbound call / web inquiry
        │
        ▼
   AI voice reception  ──►  capture details + book showing
        │
        ▼
   Lead-gen agent  ──►  qualify + score + route to CRM
        │
        ▼
   Agent gets a warm, structured lead in their pipeline
```

## The results

- **Every call answered** — 24/7 coverage with no voicemail black hole
- **Faster response** — leads routed to the right agent in minutes, not hours
- **More booked showings** — qualified inquiries flow straight to the calendar
- **Agents focused on closing** — reception and triage handled automatically
