Most small businesses lose hours every week to busywork — copying data between tools, chasing follow-ups, scheduling, and reporting. We automate those workflows so your team does the work only humans should.

## What we automate

- **Lead routing** — new inquiries get qualified, tagged, and assigned instantly
- **Follow-ups** — reminders, texts, and emails sent on schedule, every time
- **Data sync** — keep your CRM, calendar, inbox, and sheets in lockstep
- **Document work** — extract, summarize, and file information from forms and PDFs
- **Reporting** — daily digests and dashboards generated automatically

## How it works

1. **Map the workflow** — we find the repetitive steps with the clearest ROI.
2. **Connect your tools** — CRM, calendar, email, Slack, and more.
3. **Add the agent** — an AI step that decides, writes, or acts.
4. **Monitor & improve** — you get visibility and we tune the logic.

## Why process-first

We don't automate for the demo — we automate the work. That means fewer dropped leads, faster response times, and hours given back to your team every week.
