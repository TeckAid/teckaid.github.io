Got an app idea but not the time or team to build it? We use **vibe coding** — pairing generative AI with product discipline — to take you from idea to a working MVP or micro-SaaS in weeks, not quarters.

## What "vibe-coded" means here

Vibe coding is fast, but we keep it real: every build is scoped, version-controlled, and tested. You get a product you actually own, not a throwaway prototype.

- **MVP in 1–2 weeks** — a working slice you can put in front of users
- **Micro-SaaS** — multi-user apps with auth, billing, and a database
- **Generative features** — AI search, summaries, agents, and copilots baked in
- **Yours to keep** — clean code, deployed to your accounts, fully documented

## Our build process

1. **Discovery sprint** — we map the workflow, users, and the ROI.
2. **Vibe-coded prototype** — a clickable, working MVP in days.
3. **Harden & integrate** — auth, payments, your CRM, and your data.
4. **Ship & iterate** — launch, then tune from real usage.

## Great fits for a vibe-coded MVP

- Internal tools your team is duct-taping together with spreadsheets
- A niche micro-SaaS serving a specific industry or workflow
- An AI-powered customer-facing feature (quote, book, summarize, recommend)
- A faster way to test a business idea before committing big budget
