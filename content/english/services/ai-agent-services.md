## Voice reception & lead generation

Never miss a call again. Our voice agent answers in your brand's voice, captures the details that matter, qualifies the lead, and books the appointment — then drops a summary into your CRM and calendar.

- **24/7 coverage** — after-hours, weekends, and peak spikes handled automatically
- **Natural conversation** — speaks naturally, transfers to a human when needed
- **Qualify & route** — scores leads and sends hot ones straight to your team
- **CRM-ready** — every call becomes a structured record, not a voicemail

> **Proof:** For **Apex Realtors** (Houston, TX) we built a custom voice reception service and lead-gen agent that answers inbound calls around the clock and books qualified showings directly into their pipeline. [Read the case study →](/case-studies/apex-realtors/)

## How a voice agent engagement works

1. **Map the calls** — we learn your common inquiries, scripts, and hand-offs.
2. **Build & train** — we configure the agent with your business rules and knowledge.
3. **Integrate** — connect to your phone number, CRM, and calendar.
4. **Go live & tune** — we monitor calls and improve prompts from real conversations.
